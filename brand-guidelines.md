# Brand & Design Guidelines

Reference document extracted from a landing page design, for use as visual/design
context when generating a website with similar aesthetics.

## 1. Overall style

Bold, geometric, "neo-brutalist meets SaaS" style. High contrast, playful line-art
illustrations, generous whitespace, rounded rectangles everywhere. Confident and
modern, not corporate/soft.

## 2. Color palette

| Role | Approx. hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Accent (lime/acid green) | `#CBF042` – `#D6F94E` | Highlights, primary CTA on dark surfaces, card backgrounds, icon accents |
| Dark / near-black | `#191A23` | Header/footer background, dark cards, primary button fill, body headline text |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` | Page background |
| Light neutral | `#F3F3F3` | Alternate card background, form panel background |
| Body text gray | `#6C6C6C` | Paragraph/secondary text |
| Borders | `#000000` (thin, ~1–1.5px) | Card outlines on light cards, input borders |

Rule of thumb: **three-way card rotation** — light gray, acid green, near-black —
used in sequence across grids so no two adjacent cards share a background.

## 3. Typography

- **Headings:** Bold geometric sans-serif (grotesk-style — e.g. Space Grotesk,
  Archivo, or similar). Tight letter-spacing, large size (40–56px for H1),
  no italics.
- **Body text:** Same sans-serif family, regular weight, gray color, generous
  line-height. Small-to-medium size (14–16px).
- **Buttons/labels:** Same family, medium weight, sentence case (not uppercase).
- No serif fonts anywhere. No decorative fonts.

## 4. Logo & iconography

- Logo mark: a simple black geometric shape (flag/diamond-like), paired with a
  bold wordmark in the same dark color.
- Illustrations: **hand-drawn / line-art style** icons and hero graphics —
  black outlines, thin strokes, with flat lime-green fills as accents (circles,
  small shapes). Playful, slightly whimsical (sparkles, smiley faces, orbit
  lines), never photorealistic.
- Small UI icons (arrows, social icons): simple, outlined or filled in solid
  black/white circles.

## 5. Buttons

- **Primary button:** solid near-black fill, white text, fully rounded corners
  (pill or large radius ~8–12px), medium padding. No shadow, no gradient.
- **Secondary/outline button:** white/transparent fill, thin black border,
  dark text.
- **Icon buttons** (e.g. "Learn more" arrow): small circle, black or white
  background depending on card, arrow icon centered.

## 6. Cards & containers

- Large border radius (~16–24px) on all cards and containers.
- Light cards get a thin black border (~1px); dark and accent cards have no
  visible border.
- Consistent internal padding, generous (~32px).
- Grid layout: 2 columns on desktop for feature/service cards, full-bleed
  alternating color blocks.

## 7. Highlighted text ("marker" style)

A recurring, distinctive pattern: **keywords inside headings/labels get an
inline lime-green background**, like a highlighter mark, while the rest of the
text stays plain. Used for section labels (e.g. a small pill-shaped tag) and
for emphasized words inside card titles.

## 8. Accordion / list components

- Rounded rectangle rows, stacked with visible gaps (not touching).
- Active/expanded item: filled with accent lime-green background.
- Inactive items: light gray background.
- Leading large number (e.g. "01", "02") in bold, plus a circular +/− icon
  aligned to the right.

## 9. Forms

- Container: light gray rounded panel.
- Inputs: white background, thin black border, small border radius, visible
  label above each field (not just placeholder).
- Toggle/radio options styled as small pill buttons above the form, with the
  active one filled in lime green.
- Submit button: same primary dark button style as elsewhere.
- Decorative accents: black and lime sparkle/star shapes with radiating thin
  lines, placed asymmetrically near forms — purely ornamental, not functional.

## 10. Footer

- Full-width, near-black background, white text.
- Logo repeated (white version).
- Simple text link navigation, no dropdowns.
- Social icons: white circle outline, icon centered.
- Newsletter capture: input field + lime-green rounded button side by side,
  contained in a slightly lighter dark panel.

## 11. Spacing & layout principles

- Wide section padding, clear vertical rhythm between sections.
- Left-aligned text blocks paired with right-aligned illustration/graphic in
  hero sections.
- Logos/partner bar: grayscale, evenly spaced, no borders.

## 12. What to avoid

- No soft pastel colors, no drop shadows, no glassmorphism.
- No serif or script fonts.
- No sharp/square corners — everything is rounded.
- No photographic imagery — illustrations only, in the black/lime line-art
  style described above.
